# ALSO TM-B — product brief (compiled from public sources, Nov 2026)

ALSO (ridealso.com) is a micromobility company spun out of Rivian ("magical + modular micromobility"). Its first
product, the **TM-B**, is a modular e-bike that combines a cargo bike, a commuter bike and a trail bike in one
platform; the **TM-Q** extends the same platform to a four-wheel bike-lane quad for commercial cargo. The company
announced a $150M Series D and partnerships with DoorDash (autonomous delivery) and Amazon (quads in Europe/US).
Awards: TIME Best Inventions 2026, Fast Company Innovation by Design 2026, Red Dot 2026, Core77 2026.

## Riders and use
- Commuters, cargo/family riders and trail riders on one bike; swap the rear "top frame" between a solo seat ($350),
  a utility rack ($500) and a bench seat ($400). Fenders ($125). Seat/frame changes are made from the front control module.
- Classes 2–3: throttle to 20 mph, pedal assist to 28 mph (45 km/h). Country variants: 500 W (Canada), 250 W (EU).
- Payload 324 lb / 147 kg max (Launch/Performance). Hill rating 30 % grade.

## Drivetrain — pedal-by-wire series hybrid
- No chain. Pedaling drives a **generator**; the vehicle computer routes energy to the rear-wheel motor through a
  **Gates belt drive**. Pedal resistance is virtual (software-defined); an off-road mode simulates gear shifting.
- Assist multiplier 10× (Launch/Performance), 5× (Base). Wheel torque 180 Nm.
- **Regenerative braking** returns energy to the battery.

## Energy
- **800 Wh removable battery**; charges from a 240 W USB-C PD brick in ~3 h from empty.
- Bidirectional USB-C: the battery can power external devices (capped at 240 W; no native 120 V AC output).
- Range up to 100 mi / 161 km (Launch/Performance), 60 mi / 96.5 km (Base).

## Chassis
- Full suspension, 12 mm travel front and rear, adjustable. Launch/Performance: air shocks front and rear;
  Base: air front, coil rear.
- 24-inch wheels with standard off-the-shelf tires; off-road and commuter tire setups.
- Headlight, taillights and turn indicators integrated.

## HMI and connectivity
- Full-colour LED display with smartphone sync ("NEST-like" interface); companion app.
- Alphawave helmet ($250) pairs with the bike: integrated lights, four speakers, two noise-cancelling mics,
  turn-by-turn navigation from the TM-B.

## Security
- When the bike is off or reported stolen: pedals decouple, rear wheel locks, electronics shut down except
  location tracking. Auto-lock/unlock is a subscription ($15/month or $120/year).
- Public concern raised in reviews: dependence on app and servers for basic operation.

## Variants, price and timing
- Launch Edition $4,500 (spring 2026); Performance $4,500 (mid 2026); Base ≈ $3,500 (late 2026).
- Free shipping for November orders; financing and corporate sales offered; test rides at events.

## Regulatory context (to be confirmed by the agent)
- US: CPSC 16 CFR 1512 (bicycles), UL 2849 (e-bike electrical system), UL 2271 (light EV batteries),
  UN 38.3 (battery transport), FCC Part 15 (radio), state class 2/3 e-bike laws (3-class system).
- EU: EN 15194 (EPAC), Machinery Regulation, RED 2014/53/EU, EN 50604-1 (LEV batteries), 250 W / 25 km/h limit.
- Canada: 500 W / 32 km/h power-assisted bicycle rules.

## Manufacturing and service
- Contract-manufactured frames and sub-assemblies; in-house battery pack and electronics integration assumed.
- Removable battery must be serviceable without special tools; firmware OTA; returns/service pages exist.
