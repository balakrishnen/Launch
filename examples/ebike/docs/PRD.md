# E-Bike Drive Unit PRD (excerpt)
Riders: commuters 20-60, need 60 km range at 25 km/h assist, silent (<55 dBA at 1 m), water resistant IPX6, 5-year life, weight < 3.5 kg.
Fleet operators: swappable battery, telemetry over BLE/LTE, firmware OTA, theft lock.
Regulators: EN 15194 (EU), UL 2849 (US), FCC Part 15 / RED radio, UN 38.3 battery transport, IEC 62133-2 cells.
Service: diagnosable in < 10 min, no special tools for battery removal.
Manufacturing: 50k/yr, SMT + die-cast housing, end-of-line test < 90 s.
